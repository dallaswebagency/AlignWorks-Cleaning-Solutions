Commercial cleaning

One crew for the inside, the outside and the handover.

From day-to-day office upkeep to post-construction occupancy readiness, AlignWorks covers the full scope a Calgary business property needs. Pick a service to see what it includes.

Office and workspace cleaning

Reliable cleaning for offices and business spaces, booked as a one-time clean or on a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly schedule.

  • Desks, common areas and washrooms
  • Floors, baseboards and high-touch surfaces
  • Non-toxic products safe for staff

Post-construction cleaning

Dust, debris and residue removed after a build or renovation so the space is ready for occupancy and handover.

  • Construction dust and debris removal
  • Detailed wipe-down of surfaces and fixtures
  • Occupancy-ready finish

Carpet cleaning

Deep carpet cleaning that lifts dirt, allergens and stains from high-traffic floors using our own equipment.

  • Dirt and allergen removal
  • Stain treatment
  • Fresh, healthier floors

Window cleaning

Interior and exterior glass cleaned to a streak-free finish so your property looks sharp from the street and inside.

  • Interior glass
  • Exterior glass
  • Frames and sills

Exterior cleaning

Patios, siding and driveways washed down to restore curb appeal, plus gutter cleaning and gutter guard installation.

  • Patios, siding and driveways
  • Gutter cleaning
  • Gutter guard installation

Disinfection

Disinfection with air filtration to reduce bacteria, viruses and airborne contaminants in shared spaces.

  • High-touch surface disinfection
  • Air filtration
  • Healthier shared spaces
